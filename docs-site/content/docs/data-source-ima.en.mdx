---
title: "Tencent ima"
description: "Import notes, web articles, and files from Tencent ima knowledge bases."
---

ima is an AI workspace from Tencent that lets you collect notes, web pages, and files in knowledge bases. [Tencent ima website](https://ima.qq.com/)

1. Get your **Client ID** and **API Key** from [Tencent ima's open interface](https://ima.qq.com/agent-interface). In **Settings → Data sources → Tencent ima → Configure**, enter those credentials and click **Test connection**.
2. Select the knowledge bases you can access and click **Save and import**. SAG scans their subfolders and imports notes, readable web articles, and PDF, Office, Markdown, TXT, HTML, and EPUB files.
