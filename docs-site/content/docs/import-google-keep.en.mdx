---
title: "Google Keep"
description: "Import Google Keep notes from a Google Takeout ZIP file."
---

Google Keep is Google’s app for quick notes and lists. You can export notes through Google Takeout. [Google Keep product page](https://workspace.google.com/products/keep/)

1. Open [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com/), choose **Deselect all**, then select **Keep**. Choose **Next step**, select **.zip**, create the export, and download the ZIP file.

   ![Select only Keep in the Google Takeout export list](/images/google-keep-takeout-export.png)

   Screenshot source · [Notesnook Help](https://notesnook.com/help/importing-notes/import-notes-from-googlekeep)

2. In SAG Wiki desktop, [open Data sources](/en/docs/data-sources#open-data-sources) and choose **Import notes** next to **Google Keep**.
3. Click **Choose files** and select the ZIP. **Include archived notes** is on by default. You can turn it off before clicking **Import**. SAG converts the notes to Markdown and adds them to your knowledge base.

![Choose a Google Takeout ZIP and set whether to include archived notes](/images/product-source-google-keep-en.png)
