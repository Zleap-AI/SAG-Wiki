---
title: "RSS / Atom"
description: "Import articles from RSS or Atom feeds into your knowledge base."
---

RSS and Atom are feed formats that websites such as blogs and news outlets use to publish article updates. [RSS 2.0 specification](https://www.rssboard.org/rss-specification) · [Atom specification](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4287)

1. In **Settings → Data sources → RSS / Atom → Configure**, add a connection, enter a feed URL, and click **Test connection**. Press Enter or Tab in the empty address field to fill it with the example. Chinese interfaces show the [Chinese Zleap Blog feed](https://zleap.com/blog/rss.xml). Other languages show the [English feed](https://zleap.com/en/blog/rss.xml).
2. Check the feed name and destination and click **Save and import**. Files include the original link and the body or a labeled summary from the feed.
